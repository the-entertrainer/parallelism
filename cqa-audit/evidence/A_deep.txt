[H2] What is PayPal POS? 

PayPal is widely known for online payments, but it also offers in-person payment solutions. To expand beyond online checkout, PayPal introduced POS products that allow merchants to accept payments face-to-face. 
PayPal launched its first card reader, PayPal Here, in 2012. While it enabled in-person payments, evolving merchant needs and technology required a more advanced solution. 
To address this, PayPal acquired Zettle, which offers a more comprehensive POS system. Zettle provides more than just a card reader and has replaced PayPal Here as the primary in-person payment solution.

[H2] Pairing the PayPal Reader 
The PayPal Reader can be paired using the following connectivity options : 

• Bluetooth 

• USB-C cable (connecting the reader to the POS device) 

For the PayPal Reader, the Merchant can scan a QR code that will do either of the following depending on if the Merchant has the PayPal POS App installed or not: 
If PayPal POS App is Not Installed 
A setup page will open with instructions to install the app and complete the setup. 
If PayPal POS App is Installed 
The pairing process will automatically open within the PayPal POS app. 
[IMG alt=""] 
[BLOCK text] 
[H2] QR Code Pairing Steps 

Here are the steps to pair a PayPal reader using a QR code: 

• Turn on Bluetooth on the device 

• Power on the reader; a QR code will appear on the screen 

• The Merchant will be directed to the PayPal POS App to either log in or continue with the steps (If the App is not yet installed, the Merchant will be directed to the Play/App store to install it) 

• After logging in, the system will search for the reader 

• A six digit code will display on the PayPal Reader for the Merchant to compare and confirm 

• Press the confirm button on the reader; the screen will display “Reader Paired”

[BLOCK image] Alternative Pairing Method — Pair with Serial Number 
If a merchant can't pair the reader using the QR code, they can still connect their reader to their device through the Reader serial number. 
To display the serial number, press the power button on the left side of the reader. 
[IMG alt=""] 
[BLOCK text] Once the number appears, follow these steps to pair the device: 

• Ensure Bluetooth on the device is turned on 

• Merchant logs into their PayPal POS App and navigates to their settings 

• Click Connect Card Reader 

• Locate and select "PayPal Reader" 

• The PayPal Reader will then be detected 

• The Merchant confirms the three-digit code on their PayPal Reader and device 

• A six-digit code will display on the PayPal Reader for the Merchant to compare and confirm 

• Once the Merchant selects the blue confirm button in the screen of their Reader the Reader will display "Reader Paired" 

[BLOCK image] Resetting the PayPal Reader 
If the merchant's reader is not pairing or is unresponsive, perform a reset to restore it to pairing mode. 
When to reset: 

• Pairing fails or disconnects repeatedly 

• The reader is not detected in the app 

• The device becomes unresponsive 

Steps to reset: 

• Press and hold the power button (left side) and the No. 9 button at the same time 

• Hold for about five seconds until a “Please wait” message appears on the screen 

• Release the buttons and allow the reset to complete 

• Turn the reader off , then on again 

What happens after reset: 

• The reader restarts and returns to pairing mode 

• Any previous connection is cleared
